Meet Ricky Bolan

Ricky Bolan founded Bolan Septic Service in 2000, running it the way he'd want it run for his own family: show up when you say you will, tell people the truth about what their system needs, and never cut corners on a job buried in someone's yard.

Twenty-five years later, it's still a hands-on, family-run operation — no call centers, no national franchise, no subcontracted crews. Just Ricky and a small local team serving the same counties they grew up in.
